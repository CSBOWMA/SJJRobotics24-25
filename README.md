## Saint Johns Jesuits 24-25 Codebase

This repository contains the code base of the Saint Johns Jesuits robot for the INTO THE DEEP (2024-2025) competition season.

All of the team code can be found in [TeamCode](https://github.com/CSBOWMA/SJJRobotics24-25/tree/master/TeamCode/src/main/java/org/firstinspires/ftc/teamcode)

## Competition Goal

This season focused on two main types of scoring, samples and Specimens

Samples being in cut cubes of either the team color, or the neutral color. Samples needed to be placed in high up buckets to score points

Specimens are samples that have a hook attached allowing to be hung up on rungs to score points

The main challenge was grabbing the samples from the submersible in the center of the field,
as they were placed randomly in a spot that was out of reach of a standard robot

## Our Goal

The main goal of the Saint John Jesuits team was scoring samples, as our robot was most effective at getting samples at the height required to score
